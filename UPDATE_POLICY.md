# Update policy

Pin the official ELO 25 version in `manifest.yaml` (`application.version`).
A newer vendor series may be proposed by pull request after the public
documentation URL still resolves over HTTPS.

Do not mark this repository published. Qualification and catalog mutation
are maintainer operations.

Do not refresh this image by snapshotting a lab VM that already applied a
license. Tenants must supply their own `.pk` after first boot.
